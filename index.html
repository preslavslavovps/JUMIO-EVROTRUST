Why choose Evrotrust?

We are the only electronic identification scheme recognised by the EU Commission (eIDAS) that is fully remote, offers an automated solution, and can onboard individuals of any nationality.

Unlike other vendors, our eID is unique as it is just like a "Digital Passport". It is automatically accepted by all EU member states (reg. 910 eIDAS), obligatory accepted and soon used in cross-border travels. This also means that it is automatically AML compliant for KYC in all member states (5th AML Directive). The best part is that identification with eID scheme is not considered third-party reliance & we bear the risk for wrongful identification (by law).

Omnichannel Qualified Trust Service Provider (QTSP) services enable uninterrupted, seamless and user-friendly digital interactions; all our services can be white-labeled as well. We can facilitate any type of integration: web-only, mobile SDK, API integration or no-integration at all.

Have zero physical interactions with clients; start and finish each process digitally (eID & sign with QES directly in web or app). This is the process that meets truly the client expectations and unlike other solutions, Evrotrust offers the same amazing user experience to citizens from over 60 different nationalities.

Using QTSP for end-to-end customer onboarding and servicing significantly reduces operational costs and processing times. Our clients have reported: 10x cheaper to alternative processes and 15x faster with lower drop-off rates than the competition.

Digital identity verification, electronic signatures, and secure authentication methods enable swift completion of contracts, agreements, and transactions for fast growing businesses. Applicable through the whole lifecycle journey of the client.

Referral or Reseller?

Two ways to partner

Pick the model that fits how you go to market — refer clients to our sales team, or own the client and resell prepaid Trust Units bundled into your own offering.

Referral

You refer, we contract

For licensed agents and resellers of CRM, HR or other software where Evrotrust is integrated.
  • Business clients are referred to the Evrotrust sales team; Evrotrust contracts the client.
  • No volume commitment.
  • Commission fee for each signed contract and paid customer invoice.
  • No engagement with the client integration process.
  • No specific industry knowledge required.
Reseller

You own the client

For providers of software platforms bundling trust services into their own product.
  • Client ownership sits with the partner; you sign a Reseller contract with Evrotrust.
  • Purchase a prepaid Trust Unit package at reseller discounts.
  • End price is negotiated directly between you and your client.
  • One-time integration effort for embedded solutions.
  • Bundle trust services with your own business model and pricing.
